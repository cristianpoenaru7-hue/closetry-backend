Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Closetry is a digital wardrobe app with an AI stylist called Ava. This page explains what data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. Last updated: {{UPDATED}}.

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs: your account details, the wardrobe content you add, and — only if you allow it — your approximate location to check the weather.
  • If you agree to the AI features, photos of items you add, descriptions of your wardrobe and the messages you write to Ava are sent to Anthropic (Claude) to tag your clothes and suggest outfits. You are asked before anything is sent, and you can switch it off at any time.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete any item — or your entire account, photos included — from inside the app.

2. What we collect, and how

AccountName and email address, a password (stored only as a salted hash — we cannot read it), and optionally a phone number and profile photo. We also keep your theme, weather and AI preferences, and the date you accepted this policy and, if you gave it, your AI consent. You give us this directly when you register or edit your profile.

Outfits & eventsOutfits you save and the days you mark them as worn, and events you create (name, date, type, dress code and place).

Messages to usWhat you write in Contact us or Report a bug, with your name and email, plus your app and iOS version for bug reports.
Location (optional)Only if you allow it in iOS: when Ava needs the weather, your approximate coordinates are read from your device and sent to our server solely to request the current conditions. They are not saved to your account or database, and our application logs omit query strings.
Technical dataYour IP address and basic request details are processed by our hosting provider's infrastructure for security, abuse prevention and debugging, and kept for a limited time.

3. How we use your data

  • To run the app: sign you in, store and show your wardrobe, outfits and events.
  • To power Ava and automatic item tagging (with your consent — see section 4).
  • To show current weather in Ava's suggestions (only if you allow location and keep Ava uses weather on).
  • To answer your messages, fix bugs, keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To evaluate and improve the quality of Ava's suggestions: your thumbs-up / thumbs-down feedback is stored with the outfit it refers to. It is not shared with third parties.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or profiling, or share it with data brokers. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. AI features and Anthropic

Closetry uses Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, PBC (United States), for three features. Nothing is sent to Anthropic until you explicitly agree — the app asks the first time you use one of them, and you can withdraw consent at any time in Profile → Preferences → AI features.

FeatureWhat is sent to Anthropic
Automatic item taggingThe photo you have just taken or chosen, to suggest a category, colour, material, season and (if clearly legible) brand. Please keep people and faces out of the frame.
Ava (chat and event looks)Descriptions of the items in your wardrobe (category, type, colour, material, season, tags — not photos), the messages you write to Ava, and — when you ask for a look for an event — that event's name, type, dress code, place and date. The current temperature and conditions are included if weather is on.
Wardrobe insightsShort factual statements about your wardrobe (for example how many items you have in a category), to be reworded.

Never sent to Anthropic: your name, email, phone number, password, profile photo or location. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, content sent through our integration is not used to train its models; Anthropic may keep it for a limited period as described in its own policies (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). If you decline, Closetry still works as a wardrobe organiser: you enter item details yourself, and Ava is unavailable. AI suggestions can be wrong — treat them as inspiration, not advice.

6. How long we keep data, and deleting it

  • Items and outfits: kept until you delete them. Deleting an item removes its record and its photo from image storage.
  • Your account: kept until you delete it. Profile → Delete account (confirmed with your password) permanently deletes your profile, items, photos, outfits, events, conversations with Ava, insights and feedback from our database and image storage straight away. Copies cached on the image network can take a short while to expire. This can't be undone.
  • Messages to us: kept in our email inbox until we delete them, normally once the matter is resolved.
  • Server logs: kept by our hosting provider for a limited time.
  • Providers: data sent to Anthropic or OpenWeather is handled under their own retention policies, described above and on their websites.

We keep nothing about a deleted account except what the law requires us to retain.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: everything you've added is visible and editable in the app (Profile for account details).
  • Delete: items, outfits, events and your whole account, in the app. If you can't sign in, email us.
  • Withdraw consent: AI features in Profile → Preferences → AI features; weather in Preferences → Ava uses weather; location in iOS Settings → Closetry → Location; camera and photos in iOS Settings → Closetry.
  • Copy of your data: email us and we'll send you the data we hold about you.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, UK or California) you may have further rights — such as to object to or restrict processing — and the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. Write to us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Where the law requires a legal basis: we process your data to provide the service you asked for (contract), on your consent for AI features and location, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, and your sign-in token is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device. Your photos are delivered from an image network using long, random, unlisted web addresses that only your signed-in app is given — anyone who somehow had the exact address could open that one image. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to your data to what's needed to run the service.

9. Children

Closetry is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and its date, and ask for your consent again where the law or App Store rules require it.
