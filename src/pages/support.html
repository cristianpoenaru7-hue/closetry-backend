Common questions

How do I delete my account and my data?

Open Closetry, go to the Profile tab, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm with your password. This immediately and permanently removes your account, your wardrobe items and their photos, your outfits, events, conversations with Ava, saved insights and feedback. It can't be undone. If you can't sign in, email us from your registered address and we'll do it for you.

How do I delete a single item or outfit?

Tap the ⋮ menu on an item (Wardrobe) or an outfit (Outfits) and choose Delete. Deleting an item also removes its photo.

How do I turn off the AI features or the weather?

Go to Profile → Preferences. AI features controls whether Closetry may send item photos, wardrobe descriptions and your messages to Anthropic's Claude; Ava uses weather stops Ava from using the weather, and iOS Settings → Closetry → Location controls location access. Closetry still works as a wardrobe organiser with AI off — you just enter item details yourself.

I forgot my password.

Closetry doesn't have a self-service password reset yet. Email us from the address you registered with and we'll help you get back into your account.

Is my wardrobe private?

Your wardrobe is only available to your signed-in account — nothing is public or shared with other users. If you agree to the AI features, the data described in our Privacy Policy is sent to Anthropic to power them; otherwise it isn't. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

Found a bug or have an idea?

We'd like to hear it — use Profile → Report a bug in the app, or email us a short description (and a screenshot if it's visual).
